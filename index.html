Privacy Policy — Prompt Gateway

Effective date: July 9, 2026  ·  Contact: admin@promptgateways.com

Prompt Gateway is a Chrome (Manifest V3) extension that turns a spoken or typed scenario into a well-engineered prompt. This policy explains exactly what the extension handles, what it does not keep, and where data goes. It is written to match how the software actually behaves.

What the extension does

You speak or type a scenario into a side panel. The extension sends that input to your organization's Prompt Gateway service over an encrypted (TLS) connection, which asks any clarifying questions and returns an engineered prompt. The extension builds a prompt — it does not answer your topic and has no model-execution step.

What data is handled

  • Voice audio — only while you hold the microphone button (push-to-talk). Audio is captured in the browser and streamed to the Prompt Gateway to be transcribed. It is used solely to produce your prompt.
  • Prompt text — what you type, the transcript of what you say, your answers to clarifying questions, and the resulting engineered prompt.
  • Authentication data — a sign-in token from Google Identity Platform used to authenticate you to the gateway, and a client-safe copy of your organization's usage policy.

What is NOT stored

  • No transcripts, no audio, no prompts, and no results are persisted by the extension. They live only in the memory of the open side panel and are gone when you close it.
  • By default the only values the extension stores are your authentication token and the client-safe policy, and only in chrome.storage.session — an in-memory area that Chrome clears when the browser closes and that web pages cannot read. A small non-sensitive UI preference (light/dark theme) may be stored locally.
  • "Keep me signed in on this device" (optional). If — and only if — you tick this box at sign-in, the extension keeps you signed in across browser restarts by saving a single sign-in refresh token to local storage in encrypted form (it is never stored as readable text, and the encryption key stays in your browser and never leaves your device). No conversation content is ever saved. Leaving the box unticked keeps the default above (you are signed out when the browser closes). Signing out deletes the saved token and its encryption key.
  • The only way any conversation is saved to disk is if you click Save chat, which downloads a Markdown (.md) file to your own device. The extension never uploads or retains that file.

Where data goes (no third parties)

The extension communicates only with your organization's Prompt Gateway endpoint (over TLS) and Google Identity Platform endpoints (identitytoolkit.googleapis.com, securetoken.googleapis.com) for sign-in. It makes no other network calls. There are no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services, and your data is never sold. These restrictions are enforced by the extension's host_permissions and Content Security Policy. The extension does not read the web pages you visit — there is no page-reading capability of any kind.

Server-side processing (transparency note)

Once your input reaches the Prompt Gateway, it is processed by backend services to build your prompt, which includes sending prompt content to a language model that authors the final prompt. In the current configuration, server-side redaction and encryption stages are turned off for cost reasons, so prompt text reaches the prompt-authoring language model without automated redaction. A safety check still runs. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information you would not want processed by that service. The no-storage guarantees above are about this extension on your device and are unaffected.

Permissions and why they exist

  • sidePanel — draws the single side-panel interface (the only surface).
  • storage — holds the session-only auth token and policy, the local theme preference, and (only if you choose "Keep me signed in") an encrypted refresh token.
  • identity — completes the Google Identity Platform sign-in token exchange.
  • contextMenus — adds a right-click entry to open the panel.
  • Microphone — requested at runtime by Chrome when you first use push-to-talk; used only to capture your voice for transcription. No microphone permission is declared in the manifest.
  • Host permissions — limited to your gateway origin and the two Google Identity endpoints above.

Data retention and deletion

Because the extension stores no conversation content, there is nothing to delete on your device beyond closing the browser (which clears the session store), signing out (which also deletes the optional encrypted "Keep me signed in" token and its key), or removing the extension. For data handled by your organization's Prompt Gateway service, contact your organization's administrator.

Children

Prompt Gateway is a workplace/productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.
